Essential Care Instructions

TATTOO AFTERCARE & FAQ

Everything you need to know before and after your studio session.

How do I care for my tattoo during the first 24 hours?

Keep your second-skin protective film on for 24 hours unless excessive fluid builds up. Gently wash with unscented antibacterial soap and tepid water, pat dry with a clean paper towel, and apply a thin layer of specialized balm.

Can I swim or sunbathe while healing?

Avoid direct sunlight, tanning beds, ocean water, swimming pools, and saunas for at least 2 to 3 weeks during the active healing phase to protect your skin and preserve ink clarity.

What is the rescheduling policy?

You can reschedule your appointment up to 48 hours prior to your slot without losing your $100 deposit. Rescheduling under 48 hours will require a new deposit.

Are touch-ups included?

Yes! A complimentary touch-up session is offered within 6 months of your original session to ensure perfect healed lines and shading saturation.
